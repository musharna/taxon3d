Your support team asked me to send this here. I run Taxon3D (taxon3d.org), a blind pairwise arena that ranks 3D generative models on living organisms: maize, rose, lion's mane, goldfish, monarch and others, always with reference photographs of the real organism beside the pair. Rankings are Bradley-Terry with bootstrap confidence intervals.

On the text-to-3D board, Tripo H3.1 and Tripo P1 are both in the rank-1 group of 7 models, alongside Meshy v6, with the intervals overlapping (H3.1: BT 1064, 95% CI 1024-1095, 230 games; P1: BT 1038, 95% CI 1010-1082, 205 games). Head-to-head records against every opponent are on the model page:
https://taxon3d.org/models/fal:tripo-h31-text?c=team-tripo

Why this might matter to you:
- It is an independent, public result you are free to cite or share; every leaderboard vote is public with generators revealed, on Hugging Face as musharna/taxon3d-corpus-v1, and the code is MIT at github.com/musharna/taxon3d.
- The record is broken down per organism, with the prompt for each, so your team can see which taxa the models handle well and which they struggle with.
- If a newer model exists I will run it on the same tasks at no cost to you. Submissions are at taxon3d.org/submit.

If you share the result, a link back is what keeps the site findable. I am happy to answer any question about the protocol, or to talk to whoever on the research side would find the per-organism breakdown useful.
