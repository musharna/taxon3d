Subject: Meshy 6 ranks first on both boards of Taxon3D, a blind human-vote benchmark for 3D models of organisms

Hi Meshy team,

Could you forward this to your marketing or partnerships team? It is an independent benchmark result, not a support question.

I run Taxon3D (taxon3d.org), a blind pairwise arena that ranks 3D generative models on living organisms: maize, rose, lion's mane, goldfish, monarch and others, always with reference photographs of the real organism beside the pair. Rankings are Bradley-Terry with bootstrap confidence intervals, and voters never see which model made what.

Meshy is the only vendor at the top of both boards:
- Image-to-3D: Meshy 6 is rank 1 of 12 with the highest score on the board (BT 1146, 95% CI 1092-1216, 117 games), sharing the top group only with TRELLIS 2.
- Text-to-3D: Meshy v6 text is rank 1 of 7 (BT 1086, 95% CI 1044-1125, 205 games), in the top group with Tripo H3.1 and P1.

Model pages with head-to-head records against every opponent:
https://taxon3d.org/models/fal:meshy-v6?c=team-meshy
https://taxon3d.org/models/fal:meshy-v6-text?c=team-meshy

Why this might be useful to you:
- It is an independent, public result you are free to cite or share. Every leaderboard vote is public with generators revealed, on Hugging Face as musharna/taxon3d-corpus-v1, and the code is MIT at github.com/musharna/taxon3d.
- The record is broken down per organism, with the input photo or prompt for each, so your team can see exactly where the model wins and where it struggles.
- When a newer model ships I will run it on the same tasks at no cost to you. Submissions are at taxon3d.org/submit.

If you share the result, a link back is what keeps the site findable. Happy to answer any question about the protocol.
