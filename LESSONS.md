# Lessons

One line per miss: date, the class of miss, the mechanism that now catches it.

- 2026-09-16 (offline gate) A subprocess test dropped `BIO3D_DATA_DIR` to sidestep a DB-path side effect and thereby pointed the seed at the checkout's own `data/assets`; the pollution was invisible to a single run (later tests only *skipped* less) and surfaced as five failures the moment the suite ran twice in one checkout. Now: the seed subprocess always gets an isolated data dir, a test checks the checkout's `data/assets` by mtime after a seed run, and the guardrails `test` job runs the suite twice (second time offline), so first-run state that changes second-run behaviour is a red check.
- 2026-10-17 (no-fixed-wait-py) Screenshots taken on model-viewer's `loaded` carried its progress bar, which stays painted for 1.3 s after load: the 200 ms and 900 ms sleeps in the judge and thumbnail captures were shorter than that, and the spotlight render did not wait at all. Every judge contact sheet, card thumbnail and spotlight got a 5-px band, and no test looked at the pixels. Now: the template's `no-fixed-wait-py` hook refuses a `wait_for_timeout` before a measurement, and all three captures wait on `MV_SETTLED` (the bar's computed opacity), which throws if a model-viewer upgrade removes the bar. A capture with no wait at all is still invisible to the hook.
