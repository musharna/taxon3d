Coverage & governance

What is in the arena, how much evidence backs each ranking, and the rules that govern how models enter and compete. Published in full so a rank can be read with its uncertainty — not in isolation.

How models enter & compete

  • Entry — every output is added by an administrator or via the public submission queue, then moderated before it enters the arena. There is no private or preferential pre-testing: a model is not quietly trialled and published only if it scores well.
  • Fair matchmaking — pairs are drawn by a sampler that biases toward the least-compared outputs, so coverage spreads evenly rather than concentrating votes on a favoured few. Every model is drawn from the same pool by the same sampler.
  • No silent deprecation — outputs are not removed to flatter the board; the arena has no deprecation mechanism, so what competed stays on the record.
  • Exclusions are principled, not selective — only reference ground-truth scans and untextured geometry-only outputs are held out of the Mode-A perceptual pool (they confound a visual-preference vote); they remain in the objective Mode-B board. Excluded generators are flagged in the table below.
