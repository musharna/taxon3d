Procedural code-gen arena {% include "_scope_pill.html" %}

Each model authors a Blender-Python script that must run headless (Blender 4.2) and leave a valid mesh in the scene. The harness saves the result, so a model is scored on the organism it builds — never on whether it remembers our export call.

  • pass@1 — share of tasks whose first, unaided script ran and produced a valid mesh.
  • pass@repair — share that got there within two further attempts, each handed the Blender traceback from the last one. This is how people actually use these models, and the gap between the two columns is the finding: a single sample is a coin flip, so pass@1 alone understates every model on the board.
  • Morphology fidelity — share of judgeable traits the model got right. experimental: the VLM judge has not passed the Mode-C agreement (κ) gate. Treat it as a relative signal, not certified accuracy.
