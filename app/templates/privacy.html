Privacy

Bio 3D Arena stores only an anonymous session id, your votes, and coarse rate-limit metadata (e.g. timestamps used to enforce vote-frequency limits). There are no user accounts in v1 — no names, emails, or passwords are collected. We do not sell or share this data with third parties. Aggregate, de-identified vote data may be used for research and published leaderboard statistics.
